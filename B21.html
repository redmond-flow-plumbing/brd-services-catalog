Everyday Clogs: Sinks, Tubs, Toilets, and Floor Drains

The bread and butter of drain work. Kitchen sink lines clog with grease and food debris; bathroom sinks and tubs with hair and soap; toilets with — let's be honest — everything; floor drains with sediment and whatever washed in from the garage. These are usually local clogs in a single branch line, diagnosed in minutes and cleared with a professional snake or small jetter, typically within the hour. If the same fixture clogs repeatedly, though, the problem is bigger than the fixture — see below.

Main Sewer Line Cleaning and Rooter Service

When multiple fixtures back up at once, the problem is in the main line — the big pipe carrying everything to the street. Rooter service uses a heavy-duty cable machine to cut through roots and solid blockages; it's the workhorse for main lines. We always recommend a camera inspection with main-line work, because clearing a line without seeing what's in it is guessing. Common culprits in Bellevue: root intrusion through old clay joints, grease buildup in long laterals, and the occasional collapsed section that no cable will fix.

Hydro Jetting: The Deep Clean

For buildup that snaking can't remove — hardened grease, scale, sludge lining the pipe walls — hydro jetting blasts the line clean with high-pressure water, restoring full pipe diameter. It's the right call for recurring clogs, restaurant kitchen lines, and preventive maintenance on problem laterals. Jetting costs more than snaking but solves problems snaking can't, and a jetted line stays clear dramatically longer. We camera-inspect before jetting to confirm the pipe can handle the pressure.

Bellevue's rainy season makes storm drainage critical infrastructure. How to Choose the Right Service the First Time

If you're still unsure which service fits, use this rule of thumb: one fixture acting up means a branch-line service (snaking or small jetter). Several fixtures, gurgling, or any sewage means main-line service with a camera inspection. Standing water outside means storm drainage service. Kitchen problems in a business mean grease trap and commercial kitchen service. And if you're not sure at all, call and describe the symptoms — diagnosing over the phone is free, and we'd rather point you to the right service than sell you the wrong one.
